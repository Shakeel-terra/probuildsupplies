👥 Team Access

To give someone access to this admin, invite them in Supabase:

  1. Go to your Supabase project dashboard
  2. Click Authentication → Users
  3. Click Invite User
  4. Enter their email — they'll receive a link to set their password
  5. They can then log in at this admin URL with their email and password
All team members share the same product data in real time — changes made by one person are instantly visible to everyone.
