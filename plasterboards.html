8x4 or 6x3 — Which Size Do You Need?

An 8x4 board measures 2400 x 1200mm and covers 2.88m². A 6x3 board measures 1800 x 900mm and covers 1.62m² — a little over half the area. Board for board the 8x4 works out cheaper per square metre, and fewer sheets means fewer joints to tape and fill, so it is the default choice for open rooms and whole-house work.

The 6x3 earns its place on handling. A full-size 8x4 weighs around 22kg and genuinely needs two people. A 6x3 is roughly 12kg and one person can carry, lift and fix it alone. If you are boarding a loft, working up a narrow staircase, patching a ceiling or fitting out a small bathroom, the smaller sheet will save you more in effort than it costs in price per metre. Plenty of customers order both on the same delivery.

Which Plasterboard Type Do You Need?

Standard Plasterboard

The default choice for general drylining. Standard 12.5mm board suits internal partition walls, ceilings and any dry room with no specific fire or moisture requirement — bedrooms, living rooms, hallways and landings. Grey face, ivory reverse, tapered edge for jointing and skimming. £7.50 + VAT for 8x4, £5.50 + VAT for 6x3.

Fireline Plasterboard

Fireline board contains glass fibre reinforcement in the gypsum core, which holds the board together for longer when exposed to fire. You will need it wherever building regulations call for a fire rating — garage ceilings under a habitable room, party walls between dwellings, protected escape routes, stairwells, and around boilers or plant rooms. Recognisable by its pink face. At 12.5mm it typically contributes to a 30-minute fire resistance rating within a tested system, though the actual rating always depends on the full construction rather than the board alone. £10.00 + VAT for 8x4, £6.88 + VAT for 6x3.

Moisture Resistant Plasterboard

Moisture resistant board has water-repellent additives in the core, making it suitable for bathrooms, en-suites, kitchens, utility rooms and cloakrooms — anywhere humidity is high but the board is not directly wetted. Green faced, so it is easy to identify on site. Worth noting: moisture resistant board is not the same as a tile backer board. For a shower enclosure or wet room where water sits against the surface, you want a dedicated backer board instead. £10.83 + VAT, 8x4 only.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does plasterboard cost in Preston?

8x4 boards are £7.50 + VAT for standard, £10.00 + VAT for Fireline and £10.83 + VAT for moisture resistant. 6x3 boards are £5.50 + VAT for standard and £6.88 + VAT for Fireline. All boards are 12.5mm and there is no minimum order.

What sizes of plasterboard do you stock?

Two sizes. 8x4 boards measure 2400 x 1200mm and cover 2.88m² each. 6x3 boards measure 1800 x 900mm and cover 1.62m² each. Both are 12.5mm thick. Standard and Fireline are available in both sizes; moisture resistant is 8x4 only.

Should I use 8x4 or 6x3 plasterboard?

Use 8x4 for open rooms and whole-house work — it is cheaper per square metre and leaves fewer joints. Use 6x3 for lofts, narrow stairwells, small repairs and anywhere you are working alone, as a 6x3 weighs roughly 12kg against 22kg for an 8x4.

What is the difference between standard, Fireline and moisture resistant plasterboard?

Standard board is for general internal walls and ceilings. Fireline board has a glass-fibre reinforced core for fire-rated applications such as garage ceilings and party walls. Moisture resistant board has water-repellent additives for bathrooms, kitchens and utility rooms.

How much does an 8x4 plasterboard weigh?

A 2400 x 1200 x 12.5mm standard board weighs roughly 22kg. A 6x3 board of the same thickness is around 12kg. Fireline and moisture resistant boards are slightly heavier again.

Can I use moisture resistant plasterboard in a shower?

Moisture resistant board suits humid rooms but is not designed for direct water contact. For a shower enclosure or wet room, use a dedicated tile backer board behind the tiling instead.
