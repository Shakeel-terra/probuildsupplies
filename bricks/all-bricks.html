About All Facing Bricks

65mm is the standard metric brick depth used on virtually all new build and extension work in the UK since the 1970s. If your property was built in the last fifty years, this is almost certainly what you need.

Measure a course of your existing brickwork including the mortar joint before ordering. Four courses of 65mm brick with 10mm joints comes to 300mm. If yours measures noticeably more than that, you likely need our 73mm imperial range instead.

Not sure which is right? Use our free brick matching service — send a photo of your existing brickwork and we will find the closest match. Or browse all facing bricks, handmade bricks and reclaimed bricks.
