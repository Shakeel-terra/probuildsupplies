About Rustic Bricks

Rustic bricks carry pronounced texture, creasing and colour variation within each brick. They produce walls with genuine depth and shadow, the opposite of a flat uniform elevation.

These suit barn conversions, cottage extensions and anywhere a wall should look like it has always been there. Variation is the point, so expect noticeable differences between individual bricks.

Not sure which is right? Use our free brick matching service — send a photo of your existing brickwork and we will find the closest match. Or browse all facing bricks, handmade bricks and reclaimed bricks.
