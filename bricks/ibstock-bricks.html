About Ibstock Bricks

Ibstock is one of the UK’s largest brick manufacturers, and its bricks are found on housing, extensions and commercial buildings across the country. We stock bricks from many of its ranges, including Tradesman, Leicester, Parkhouse, Chesterton, Ashdown, Eclipse and Throckley, in 65mm metric and selected 68mm and 73mm imperial sizes.

Every price is per pack including VAT, with the price per brick shown underneath. Delivery is by pallet and priced by postcode at checkout. Not sure which brick matches your wall? Use our free brick matching service or order samples, any 3 for £7.99.
