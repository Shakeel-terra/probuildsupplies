About Wienerberger Bricks

Wienerberger is one of the largest brick makers in the world, with brickworks across the UK. We stock bricks from its Warnham, Waresley, Denton, Ewhurst, Hartlebury, Sandown, Todhill and Smeed Dean ranges, plus its Continental range and Staffordshire blue engineering bricks.

Every price is per pack including VAT, with the price per brick shown underneath. Delivery is by pallet and priced by postcode at checkout. Not sure which brick matches your wall? Use our free brick matching service or order samples, any 3 for £7.99.
