About Conservation Bricks

Conservation and listed building work often carries planning conditions specifying reclaimed or heritage-appropriate brick. Our reclaimed range and heritage blends are built for exactly this.

Keep your order confirmation and delivery paperwork. Planning and building control officers occasionally ask for evidence of what was actually supplied, particularly where reclaimed material was a condition of consent.

Not sure which is right? Use our free brick matching service — send a photo of your existing brickwork and we will find the closest match. Or browse all facing bricks, handmade bricks and reclaimed bricks.
